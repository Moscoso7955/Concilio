Privacy Policy

Arca · Owner Portal · Last updated September 10, 2026

Arca (conciliowealth.com) is a business application. Anyone can create a workspace; the owners, partners and staff invited into a workspace use it to review the business information they share with each other. Each workspace's data is visible only to its members.

What we collect

  • Account information. Your name and email address, used solely to sign you in and control what you can see. Sign-in is by emailed magic link or Google sign-in; with Google we receive only your basic profile (name, email) and never your password.
  • Business records. Financial statements, documents, invoices, and related operating data that portal members share with each other as part of running the business.
  • Activity records. Basic sign-in and usage events, kept for security and audit purposes.
  • Connected accounts. If a workspace admin links a unit to QuickBooks Online, we store the access tokens needed to read that company's profit and loss report on request. Tokens are never visible in the portal and are removed when the link is deleted.
  • Billing. Payments are handled by Stripe. We keep a Stripe customer reference and subscription status; card and bank details are held by Stripe, never by Arca.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for their own use.
  • We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking of any kind. Cookies and similar storage are used only to keep you signed in.

Service providers

The portal runs on infrastructure providers acting on our behalf: Supabase (authentication and database), Vercel (hosting), Google (optional sign-in), and Resend (transactional email). Each processes data only as needed to provide its service.

Retention and access

Account and business records are kept for as long as they are needed for the business and its legal obligations. Access is limited by role, and every member sees only what their ownership or role permits.
