Callidus Owner Portal

The private management application for owners and operators of Callidus Co. entities.

What it is

Callidus Owner Portal is an internal, invitation-only web application operated by Callidus Co. (Callidus Holdings LLC and its affiliated hospitality entities). It gives approved owners, partners, and management staff a single place to review financial reports, share documents and memos, track invoices, manage operations and tasks, and view ownership records across the Callidus portfolio of venues.

Who can use it

Access is restricted to individuals who have been added to the portal's authorized user list by a Callidus Co. administrator. The portal is not open to the general public.

How sign-in works

Users sign in with their Google Account. The portal requests only your basic profile information (name and email address) in order to identify you and match you to your authorized role. No other Google data is requested, stored, or shared. If your Google Account is not on the authorized list, you will see a "not authorized for portal access" message and no data will be retained.

Features

  • Reports — financial and operational reporting by entity
  • Memo Board — announcements and notes for owners and management
  • Management — time tracking, tasks, and organization chart
  • Documents — shared agreements, policies, and records
  • Invoices — billing and payment status
  • Marketing and Site Content — brand and web content management
  • Ownership — capitalization and ownership records
