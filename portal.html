Arca — Portfolio Management

The portal for owners and operators who run more than one entity.

What it is

Arca is a web application for managing a portfolio of companies from one place. Each customer gets a private workspace with financial reports, shared documents and memos, bill filing with intercompany settle-ups, operations tasks and time tracking, and an ownership map of every entity and who holds it.

Who can use it

Anyone can start a workspace with a free trial; after that a workspace is a paid monthly subscription. Members join a workspace only when its administrator invites them. Data in one workspace is never visible to another.

How sign-in works

Users sign in with their Google Account or an email link. The portal requests only basic profile information (name and email address) in order to identify you and match you to your workspace and role. No other Google data is requested, stored, or shared.

Features

  • Monthly reports per entity, with QuickBooks Online sync where connected
  • Documents, memos and distribution notices
  • Bills filed by email or upload, with intercompany allocation and month-end settlements
  • Tasks, recurring work, time tracking and a team overview
  • An ownership map of entities, individuals and their stakes
